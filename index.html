Common Questions

Everything you need to know before your first deal.

Are the rates fixed or negotiable? +
Rates may vary based on volume and real-time market conditions. The listed rates are indicative. For large or bulk transactions, better rates can be negotiated directly. Contact us before initiating any deal.
Is there a minimum transaction amount? +
We handle transactions of all sizes. However, bulk rates (₹110/USD) kick in for deals exceeding $1,000 equivalent. Flash service rates apply to flash token transactions regardless of size.
How long does a transaction take? +
Standard OTC deals are typically completed within a few minutes of confirmation. Flash service transactions are near-instant. Network congestion and verification may occasionally add a small delay.
Which tokens and networks are supported? +
We support all major tokens including USDT, BTC, ETH, SOL, TRX, BNB, and many more across BEP20, ERC20, TRC20, SOL, and TON networks. Flash services cover 9+ wallets with 25+ token types.
How do I know my funds are safe? +
We operate with full transparency — you confirm the rate and address before any transfer. We do not hold your funds beyond the time required to execute the trade. Our team has a strong track record of safe, successful transactions.
What is a Flash Service? +
Flash services are specialized token operations performed on wallets like Trust Wallet, MetaMask, Binance Web3, and others. These appear as standard token transfers and are priced separately at ₹20/USD (bulk ₹15/USD). Contact us for full details.
